## Ingredients

- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon chipotle chili pepper, (more or less to taste)
- 1/2 tsp EACH ground cumin, smoked paprika, onion powder, garlic powder
- 1 pound chicken breasts, (chopped into 1-inch pieces)
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- chopped cilantro for garnish ((optional))

## Method

1. Spice mix: Whisk all the spices together in a medium bowl.
2. Season chicken: While the chopped chicken is still on the cutting board, pat it dry with paper towels, then toss it with the spice mix.
3. Cook chicken: Heat 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil in a large nonstick skillet or enameled cast iron pan over medium-high heat. Once smoking, add chicken in a single layer. Sear chicken for about 3 minutes, then flip over.  Continue to cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, or just until cooked through.
4. Add glaze: Stir in the lime juice and honey until evenly coated. (start with 1 TBS honey if you want it less sweet),  Serve with all that yummy glaze.  Garnish with cilantro if desired.
5. Serve:  See post for lots of tasty serving ideas!
