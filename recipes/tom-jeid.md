## Ingredients

- 1 L light pork or chicken stock
- 200 g minced pork or sliced meat
- Soft tofu, cubed
- Glass noodles (woon sen), soaked
- 1–2 eggs
- Garlic, white pepper, fish sauce / soy
- Spring onion, coriander
- Optional: cabbage or Chinese greens

## Method

1. Simmer stock; season lightly.
2. Cook meat; add tofu and greens.
3. Add soaked glass noodles briefly so they don’t over-soften.
4. Stir in beaten egg in a thin stream for ribbons, or poach eggs whole.
5. Finish with herbs. Critique note: noodles last; soup should stay clear and gentle.
