## Ingredients

- 1-2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 150 g ground pork
- 5 shiitake mushrooms, thinly sliced
- kosher salt, to taste
- 3 green onions, thinly sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 1 shallot, finely diced
- 2.5 cm ginger, finely grated
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tsp doubanjiang
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 360 ml dashi or chicken stock
- 960 ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp toasted white sesame seeds, lightly ground
- 50 g red miso
- 1 large egg
- 113 g dry ramen noodles
- corn kernels, to serve (from 1 cob)
- pickled ginger, optional
- 2-4 tsp chilli oil

## Method

1. Optional dashi: steep kombu in nearly boiling water 5 minutes, add bonito flakes off heat 8–10 minutes, strain.
2. Brown ground pork in oil until crumbly and golden. Add mushrooms and salt; cook until softened.
3. Add onion whites, shallot and ginger; cook 1–2 minutes. Stir in sugar, sesame oil, white pepper, doubanjiang and mirin for 1 minute.
4. Add dashi (if using) and chicken stock; bring to a boil. Stir in sesame seeds; simmer 3–4 minutes.
5. Off heat, press miso through a mesh strainer into the broth and stir until smooth. Keep warm.
6. Soft-boil egg 6 minutes; ice bath; peel and halve.
7. Cook ramen noodles per packet. Ladle broth over noodles; top with egg, corn, green onion greens, pickled ginger and chilli oil.
