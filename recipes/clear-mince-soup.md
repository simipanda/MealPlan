## Ingredients

- 300 g minced beef or pork
- 1 L light stock or water + seasoning
- Garlic, crushed
- White pepper
- Soy sauce or fish sauce
- Soft tofu cubes (optional)
- Spring onion / coriander
- Optional: glass noodles or soft vegetables (cabbage, marrow)

## Method

1. Bring stock to a simmer with garlic.
2. Drop in mince in small clumps or pre-seasoned meatballs; poach gently.
3. Add tofu/veg; season clear and savoury (fish sauce + white pepper).
4. Finish with spring onion. Critique note: keep broth clear — don’t boil hard after adding mince.
