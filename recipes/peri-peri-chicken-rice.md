## Ingredients

- 2 tsp sunflower oil
- 600 g skinless boneless chicken breast, bite-sized
- 2 tsp peri peri seasoning
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 red onion, peeled and chopped
- 2 red peppers, deseeded and chopped
- 800 ml hot chicken stock
- 3 tbsp tomato purée
- 200 g long grain rice, rinsed
- 200 g frozen peas
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Brown chicken pieces in oil over medium heat about 5 minutes.
2. Add peri peri seasoning, garlic and onion; fry 3–5 minutes until onion softens.
3. Add peppers, stock, tomato purée, rice, salt and pepper. Cover, low heat, 15 minutes.
4. Stir in peas; cover and cook 5 minutes more until rice and chicken are done.
